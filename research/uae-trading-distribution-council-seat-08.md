   QUOTE: "Uncertified suppliers: a UAE mainland supplier without a certificate counts as 10%, and any other supplier without one counts as 0%."

   DATE: 2026-10-04
   STRENGTH: SECONDARY (restates MoIAT 2025 certification guidelines; MoIAT's own formula page at https://moiat.gov.ae/en/programs/icv-formula shows "Value of Purchase x ICV of supplier")
